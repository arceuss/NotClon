yarg_grain.png is NOT a YARG asset. It is Unity's URP "Medium01" film grain
texture, from:

    Packages/com.unity.render-pipelines.core/Runtime/Textures/FilmGrain/Medium01.png
    (package version 17.3.0, Unity 6000.3)

Source: github.com/Unity-Technologies/Graphics, ref 6000.3/staging.
Unity Companion License. It is loaded as the film grain lookup for the YARG
engine style's post chain (UberPP order: bloom -> grade -> grain -> ACES),
sampled on the alpha channel via a GL texture swizzle at upload.
